# Merge scenarios: results

_Generated by `spikes/merge-scenarios/` (`npx vitest run --config spikes/merge-scenarios/vitest.config.ts`). Don't edit by hand. The reading of these results is in [`merge-scenarios.md`](merge-scenarios.md)._

Two people, Alice and Bob, start on the same ten-card board and edit it through the app's real commands. "Live" means both edit within the same second or so, before either sees the other's change. "Offline" means one of them works disconnected for a while. Then their documents sync.

Every scenario runs twice, with the two people's Yjs client IDs swapped. Client IDs are random per session, so a result that changes when they're swapped is a coin toss that nobody chose.

**Verdicts:** 9 as expected, 5 coin toss, 3 surprising, 4 work lost, 5 bad state. Every scenario converged: both people see the same board afterwards (yes).

## Each scenario

### L1: Both drag one card to different quarters

- **Alice:** Drags *Invoice redesign* from Q1 to Q2.
- **Bob:** Drags *Invoice redesign* from Q1 to Q3.
- **Expected:** One of them wins, probably the later drop, and the card is in one quarter.
- **Result:** The board shows Q2, but the document keeps Q2, Q3 until the card is next moved. The value shown is the one with the lowest ID, not the later drop.
- **Verdict:** bad state

### L2: Both drag one card to different areas

- **Alice:** Drags *Invoice redesign* from Billing to Identity.
- **Bob:** Drags *Invoice redesign* from Billing to Platform.
- **Expected:** The card ends up in one of the two areas.
- **Result:** The card is in Identity, Platform: both moves landed, because System holds several values. Nobody put it in two areas.
- **Verdict:** surprising

### L3: Both drag one card to different sequence columns

- **Alice:** In Sequence, drags *Audit log export* to the first column.
- **Bob:** In Sequence, drags *Audit log export* to the second column.
- **Expected:** One of them wins.
- **Result:** The card is in the second column. Sequence is one value, so one drop wins outright.
- **With client IDs swapped:** The card is in the first column. Sequence is one value, so one drop wins outright.
- **Verdict:** coin toss

### L4: Two people change different things on one card

- **Alice:** Drags *Rate limits* from Q3 to Q2.
- **Bob:** Sizes *Rate limits* L in the inspector.
- **Expected:** Both changes stick.
- **Result:** The card is in Q2, sized L.
- **Verdict:** as expected

### L5: Both rename one card

- **Alice:** Renames *Audit log export* to "Audit log export (CSV)".
- **Bob:** Renames *Audit log export* to "Audit trail export".
- **Expected:** One title wins, whole.
- **Result:** The title is "Audit trail export".
- **With client IDs swapped:** The title is "Audit log export (CSV)".
- **Verdict:** coin toss

### L6: One renames a card while the other deletes it

- **Alice:** Renames *Rate limits* to "API rate limits".
- **Bob:** Deletes *Rate limits*.
- **Expected:** The card is gone; Alice sees it disappear.
- **Result:** The card is gone, and so is the rename.
- **Verdict:** as expected

### L7: Both put one card into different groups

- **Alice:** Puts *Audit log export* inside *Passwordless login*.
- **Bob:** Puts *Audit log export* inside *Invoice redesign*.
- **Expected:** It ends up in one of them.
- **Result:** It's inside Invoice redesign.
- **With client IDs swapped:** It's inside Passwordless login.
- **Verdict:** coin toss

### L8: Each puts a card inside the other's

- **Alice:** Puts *Tax engine migration* inside *Invoice redesign*.
- **Bob:** Puts *Invoice redesign* inside *Tax engine migration*.
- **Expected:** One nest wins; a card can't be inside itself (requirement 14).
- **Result:** *Tax engine migration* shows at top level (on a cycle), and *Invoice redesign* at top level (on a cycle). The document holds a loop of parents, which the board shows as two loose cards. Neither nest survived, and nothing says why.
- **Verdict:** bad state

### L9: One deletes a group while the other adds a card inside it

- **Alice:** Deletes *Passwordless login*, with its three stories.
- **Bob:** Adds "WebAuthn fallback" inside *Passwordless login*.
- **Expected:** Either the new card goes with the group, or the delete is held back.
- **Result:** The group and its three stories are gone. "WebAuthn fallback" survives, at top level (its group was deleted), with no sign of where it came from.
- **Verdict:** surprising

### L10: One deletes a group while the other moves a card into it

- **Alice:** Deletes *Passwordless login*.
- **Bob:** Puts *Audit log export* inside *Passwordless login*.
- **Expected:** The moved card survives somewhere.
- **Result:** *Audit log export* shows at top level (its group was deleted): it wasn't deleted, because Alice's delete didn't know it was inside. Its parent field still names the deleted group.
- **Verdict:** as expected

### L11: One deletes a group while the other edits a card inside it

- **Alice:** Deletes *Passwordless login*.
- **Bob:** Renames *Account recovery* and drags it to Q2.
- **Expected:** The card goes with its group; Bob sees it disappear.
- **Result:** The card is gone, with Bob's rename and move.
- **Verdict:** as expected

### L12: One links to a card the other deletes, then the delete is undone

- **Alice:** Links *Rate limits* before *Audit log export*.
- **Bob:** Deletes *Audit log export*, then undoes the delete.
- **Expected:** The link goes with the card, and comes back if the card does.
- **Result:** While the card is deleted, the link is hidden. After Bob's undo, the card is back and the link is back too.
- **Verdict:** as expected

### L13: Both start a new sequence column in the same gap

- **Alice:** In Sequence, double-clicks the gap after the last column in Platform, and types "Usage dashboard".
- **Bob:** Does the same in the same gap, and types "Quota alerts".
- **Expected:** Two new cards, in one new column or two.
- **Result:** Both cards are in one new column, since both people computed the same key for the gap.
- **Verdict:** as expected

### L14: One deletes a quarter while the other drags a card into it

- **Alice:** Deletes Q3 in Properties. Its cards lose their quarter (Q4).
- **Bob:** Drags *Invoice redesign* into Q3.
- **Expected:** Q3 is gone, and every card that was in it, Bob's too, is undated.
- **Result:** *Invoice redesign* holds a quarter that no longer exists (deleted value q3), so Roadmap shows it in No quarter × Billing. The other Q3 cards are undated, as Alice expected. If Alice undoes, Q3 comes back with Bob's card in it.
- **Verdict:** bad state

### L15: Both add an area with the same name

- **Alice:** Adds an area "Data" from "+ Add area".
- **Bob:** Adds an area "Data" from "+ Add area".
- **Expected:** One area called Data.
- **Result:** There are 2 areas called Data. Each has a random ID, so they never merge, and the "name already used" check ran before either saw the other's.
- **Verdict:** surprising

### L16: One renames an area while the other reorders it

- **Alice:** Renames Platform to "Platform services".
- **Bob:** Moves Platform up a place, above Identity.
- **Expected:** Both changes stick.
- **Result:** The area is called "Platform" and is above Identity. A value's name, parent and position are written as one object, so one of the two edits is dropped.
- **With client IDs swapped:** The area is called "Platform services" and is below Identity. A value's name, parent and position are written as one object, so one of the two edits is dropped.
- **Verdict:** work lost

### L17: One ungroups a group while the other adds a card inside it

- **Alice:** Ungroups *Passwordless login*: its stories move up a level.
- **Bob:** Adds "WebAuthn fallback" inside *Passwordless login*.
- **Expected:** The new card moves up with its siblings.
- **Result:** "WebAuthn fallback" shows at top level (its group was deleted), like its former siblings. The link from *Passkey enrolment* was re-pointed at the cards Alice knew about, not at the new one.
- **Verdict:** as expected

### L18: Both give an untagged card different areas

- **Alice:** Drags *Release notes* from "No area" into Billing.
- **Bob:** Drags *Release notes* from "No area" into Identity.
- **Expected:** The same as L2: both land, since System holds several values. Or one wins.
- **Result:** The card is in Identity only. A card's first value for a property is written as a new set, and two new sets for one property replace each other, unlike L2, where both people edit a set that already exists.
- **With client IDs swapped:** The card is in Billing only. A card's first value for a property is written as a new set, and two new sets for one property replace each other, unlike L2, where both people edit a set that already exists.
- **Verdict:** coin toss

### U1: Undo reverses only my own edit

- **Alice:** Drags *Rate limits* from Q3 to Q2.
- **Bob:** Renames *Rate limits* to "API rate limits". Then Alice presses ⌘Z.
- **Expected:** The card goes back to Q3, and keeps Bob's title.
- **Result:** The card is in Q3, titled "API rate limits".
- **Verdict:** as expected

### U2: I undo a move someone has since moved again

- **Alice:** Drags *Invoice redesign* from Q1 to Q2.
- **Bob:** Sees it in Q2, and drags it on to Q3. Then Alice presses ⌘Z.
- **Expected:** Either nothing happens, since Bob has moved it since, or it goes back to Q1. Not both.
- **Result:** The board shows Q1, and the document holds Q1, Q3. Alice's undo put Q1 back without taking Bob's Q3 away.
- **Verdict:** bad state

### O1: An hour of overlapping drags

- **Alice:** Offline, moves four cards to new quarters.
- **Bob:** Online meanwhile, moves two of the same cards to other quarters.
- **Expected:** Every card is in one quarter. Whoever moved a card last, in real time, probably wins.
- **Result:** 2 cards hold two quarters (Invoice redesign, Audit log export), and show the one with the lower ID: *Invoice redesign* in Q2, *Audit log export* in Q1. Who moved last plays no part.
- **Verdict:** bad state

### O2: Opening a plan file while offline

- **Alice:** Online, adds "Usage dashboard" and renames *Audit log export*.
- **Bob:** Offline, opens a plan file: the same plan, saved yesterday, with the same card IDs.
- **Expected:** Bob's file replaces the board for everyone, or Alice's work survives on top. Not a mix.
- **Result:** The board holds the file's cards plus Alice's new "Usage dashboard". Alice's rename is lost: opening a file deletes every card and writes it again, which throws away edits to cards Bob's copy knew about, but not cards it didn't.
- **Verdict:** work lost

### O3: Starting a new blank plan while offline

- **Alice:** Online, adds "Usage dashboard" in Q2 × Platform.
- **Bob:** Offline, chooses File › New blank plan, meaning to start something else.
- **Expected:** Bob gets a blank plan of his own. Alice's board is untouched.
- **Result:** Both boards are now blank except for "Usage dashboard", whose quarter and area no longer exist (it holds deleted value q2). One person's "new plan" wiped the shared one for everyone.
- **Verdict:** work lost

### O4: Deleting a group someone is refining offline

- **Alice:** Offline, adds two stories inside *Passwordless login*, renames *Passkey enrolment*, and links it before *Rate limits*.
- **Bob:** Online meanwhile, deletes *Passwordless login* as out of scope.
- **Expected:** Alice is told what happened to her work.
- **Result:** Alice's two new stories survive as loose cards ("WebAuthn fallback", "Device trust"), at the top level. Her rename and her link went with the deleted group. Nothing tells her which.
- **Verdict:** work lost

### O5: Two reorganisations of the same cards

- **Alice:** Offline, groups *Invoice redesign* and *Tax engine migration* into a new group.
- **Bob:** Offline too, groups *Tax engine migration* and *Seat sync from directory* into a new group.
- **Expected:** The groups merge, or one wins.
- **Result:** Both groups exist. *Tax engine migration* is inside Bob's group, so the other group has one card left.
- **With client IDs swapped:** Both groups exist. *Tax engine migration* is inside Alice's group, so the other group has one card left.
- **Verdict:** coin toss

### O6: Opposite links while apart

- **Alice:** Offline, links *Seat sync from directory* before *Tax engine migration*.
- **Bob:** Links *Tax engine migration* before *Seat sync from directory*.
- **Expected:** A loop, flagged, as when one person makes one (Q37).
- **Result:** Both links exist, and the board flags the loop.
- **Verdict:** as expected
